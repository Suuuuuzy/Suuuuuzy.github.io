I earned my Ph.D. in Computer Science at Johns Hopkins University , advised by Prof. Yinzhi Cao. My research focuses on security and privacy in web and mobile applications, using traditional program analysis and LLM techniques, that reason across complex architectures spanning multiple execution layers, language boundaries, and trust domains. I have received two Distinguished Paper Awards (IEEE S&P 2025, ACM CCS 2023) and a Best Paper Honorable Mention (USENIX Security 2025). My work has resulted in 30+ CVEs, including widely used libraries and applications maintained by Google and Microsoft, as well as bug bounties from Meta, Vercel, and ByteDance. Before joining JHU, I obtained my bachelor's degree at Computer Science Department of Zhejiang University in 2020, where I was a member of ACEE, Chu Kochen College .
